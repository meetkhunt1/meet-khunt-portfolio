That is the whole brief. No wireframes, no copy doc, no image shoot.

  1. Input 1

    Reference page

    An advertorial or listicle whose structure you want to follow: the flow, the sections, the persuasion order.

  2. Input 2

    Product page

    Your own product page, so the system knows the product, the offer, the benefits and the proof it can use.

  3. Input 3

    Marketing angle

    The hook you want to test: the problem, the audience or the promise this version of the page is built around.

Claude handles the rest.

  • ✦

    Recreates the reference

    The page structure is rebuilt section by section: headline, story, proof, offer and call to action, in the same order.

  • ✦

    Adapts it to your product

    Every section is rewritten around your product and your angle, using what is on your product page.

  • ✦

    Generates the images

    Relevant images are generated for each section, so the draft is complete instead of full of placeholders.

  • ✦

    Stays easy to edit

    Changing a headline, a section or the whole angle is a quick edit and a rerun, not a rebuild by hand.
