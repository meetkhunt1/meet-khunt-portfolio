2d:T59e7,<p>Subscription-based purchasing is an important part of many DTC eCommerce businesses, especially in health, wellness, supplements, skincare, and consumable products.</p>
<p>Checkout Champ provides subscription and recurring billing functionality through its campaign and product configuration. However, setting up the recurring offer is only one part of the implementation.</p>
<p>You also need to give customers a way to select a subscription option on the frontend.</p>
<p>In this guide, we&#39;ll walk through how to add subscription options to Checkout Champ funnel pages using its native page builder elements, configure Subscribe &amp; Save functionality, customize the frontend experience, and test the complete subscription flow.</p>
<h2 id="1-understand-how-subscription-setup-works-in-checkout-champ">1. Understand How Subscription Setup Works in Checkout Champ<a class="prose-anchor" href="#1-understand-how-subscription-setup-works-in-checkout-champ" aria-label="Link to this section: 1. Understand How Subscription Setup Works in Checkout Champ">#</a></h2>
<p>Before editing the frontend, it&#39;s important to understand how the subscription setup is connected.</p>
<p>A typical subscription implementation involves three parts:</p>
<p><strong>1. Base Product and Campaign Offer</strong></p>
<p>The product must have an appropriate recurring offer configured in the Checkout Champ campaign.</p>
<p><strong>2. Subscription Configuration</strong></p>
<p>The recurring offer defines the subscription price, billing frequency, and other billing settings.</p>
<p><strong>3. Frontend Subscription Selection</strong></p>
<p>The page builder provides elements that allow customers to choose between a one-time purchase and a recurring subscription.</p>
<figure class="prose-figure"><img src="/images/blog/checkout-champ-subscription-parts.webp" alt="Diagram of the three parts of a Checkout Champ subscription setup: one-time and recurring campaign offers sharing a Base Product, the recurring offer's price and billing frequency, and a frontend selector choosing between the two offers" width="1600" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Illustration: the frontend element selects between offers that already exist in the campaign.</figcaption></figure>
<p>The frontend element doesn&#39;t independently create a subscription. It works with the recurring offers already configured in the campaign.</p>
<h2 id="2-prerequisites-configure-your-subscription-offers">2. Prerequisites: Configure Your Subscription Offers<a class="prose-anchor" href="#2-prerequisites-configure-your-subscription-offers" aria-label="Link to this section: 2. Prerequisites: Configure Your Subscription Offers">#</a></h2>
<p>Before adding a subscription selector to your page, make sure the campaign has the required product configuration.</p>
<p>In Checkout Champ, navigate to:</p>
<p><strong>CRM → Campaigns → Edit Campaign → Campaign Products</strong></p>
<p>Create or configure the relevant offers.</p>
<p>For a basic Subscribe &amp; Save setup, you&#39;ll need:</p>
<ul>
<li>A one-time purchase offer.</li>
<li>A recurring subscription offer.</li>
<li>The same Base Product assigned to both offers.</li>
<li>An active and appropriate Gateway Account.</li>
<li>The correct product pricing and billing schedule.</li>
</ul>

<p>The recurring offer determines how frequently the customer is billed.</p>
<p>You can configure different recurring schedules depending on your offer requirements, such as every 30, 60, or 90 days.</p>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--important" role="note" aria-label="Important"><p class="prose-callout__label">Important</p><p>Both offers must share the same Base Product when using Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles.</p>
</aside>
<h2 id="3-method-1-add-the-subscribe-element-to-a-product-page">3. Method 1: Add the Subscribe Element to a Product Page<a class="prose-anchor" href="#3-method-1-add-the-subscribe-element-to-a-product-page" aria-label="Link to this section: 3. Method 1: Add the Subscribe Element to a Product Page">#</a></h2>
<p>Checkout Champ Page Builder 2.0 includes a native Subscribe element for product detail pages.</p>
<p>This is useful when you want customers to choose between a one-time purchase and a subscription while viewing the product.</p>
<h3 id="step-1-open-the-product-detail-page">Step 1: Open the Product Detail Page<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-1-open-the-product-detail-page" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 1: Open the Product Detail Page">#</a></h3>
<ol>
<li>Navigate to Funnel Builder.</li>
<li>Open the relevant funnel.</li>
<li>Edit your Product Detail page.</li>
<li>Open the Building Blocks panel.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="step-2-find-the-subscribe-element">Step 2: Find the Subscribe Element<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-2-find-the-subscribe-element" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 2: Find the Subscribe Element">#</a></h3>
<p>Navigate to:</p>
<p><strong>Basic Elements → Products → Subscribe</strong></p>
<p>Drag the Subscribe element onto your product page.</p>
<p>Some Product Detail templates already include this element by default, so check the existing page before adding another one.</p>
<h3 id="step-3-customize-the-appearance">Step 3: Customize the Appearance<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-3-customize-the-appearance" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 3: Customize the Appearance">#</a></h3>
<p>You can adjust the styling to match your store design.</p>
<p>For example, you could display:</p>
<ul>
<li>One-time purchase</li>
<li>Subscribe &amp; Save</li>
<li>Subscription price</li>
<li>Available subscription information</li>
</ul>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--warning" role="note" aria-label="Warning"><p class="prose-callout__label">Warning</p><p>Make sure you preserve the original elements and classes of the native Subscribe element. You can add custom classes for styling, but don&#39;t remove the existing functionality.</p>
</aside>
<h3 id="step-4-save-and-publish">Step 4: Save and Publish<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-4-save-and-publish" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 4: Save and Publish">#</a></h3>
<p>Save the page and publish your funnel.</p>
<p>Visit the live page and check whether the subscription option appears.</p>
<p>The Subscribe element automatically displays the available product names and prices when the associated Base Product has a recurring option. If no recurring option is available, the element is hidden.</p>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--tip" role="note" aria-label="Tip"><p class="prose-callout__label">Tip</p><strong>When should you use this method?</strong></p>
<p>Use the native Subscribe element when you want customers to select a subscription directly from a product detail page.</p>
</aside>
<h2 id="4-method-2-add-a-subscribe-and-save-checkbox-to-checkout">4. Method 2: Add a Subscribe &amp; Save Checkbox to Checkout<a class="prose-anchor" href="#4-method-2-add-a-subscribe-and-save-checkbox-to-checkout" aria-label="Link to this section: 4. Method 2: Add a Subscribe &amp; Save Checkbox to Checkout">#</a></h2>
<p>Another common implementation is to let customers upgrade their one-time purchase to a subscription directly on the checkout page.</p>
<p>For this method, Checkout Champ provides a specific checkbox configuration.</p>
<h3 id="step-1-confirm-your-one-time-and-recurring-offers">Step 1: Confirm Your One-Time and Recurring Offers<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-1-confirm-your-one-time-and-recurring-offers" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 1: Confirm Your One-Time and Recurring Offers">#</a></h3>
<p>Make sure both offers exist in the same campaign and share the same Base Product.</p>
<p>For example:</p>
<ul>
<li>One-time purchase: $49.99</li>
<li>Subscription: $39.99 every 30 days</li>
</ul>
<p>If you have multiple products with different subscription frequencies, configure the appropriate Base Product relationships for each offer pair.</p>
<h3 id="step-2-open-the-checkout-page">Step 2: Open the Checkout Page<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-2-open-the-checkout-page" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 2: Open the Checkout Page">#</a></h3>
<ol>
<li>Go to Funnel Builder.</li>
<li>Open your campaign funnel.</li>
<li>Edit the checkout page.</li>
<li>Open the Building Blocks panel.</li>
</ol>
<h3 id="step-3-add-a-form-checkbox">Step 3: Add a Form Checkbox<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-3-add-a-form-checkbox" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 3: Add a Form Checkbox">#</a></h3>
<p>Navigate to the Forms section and drag a Checkbox element onto the checkout page.</p>
<p>Select the checkbox and open its settings in the Style Manager.</p>
<p>Set the checkbox ID to exactly:</p>
<p><code>Subscribe</code></p>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--important" role="note" aria-label="Important"><p class="prose-callout__label">Important</p><p>This ID is important because Checkout Champ uses it to recognize the Subscribe &amp; Save checkbox functionality.</p>
</aside>
<h3 id="step-4-customize-the-checkbox">Step 4: Customize the Checkbox<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-4-customize-the-checkbox" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 4: Customize the Checkbox">#</a></h3>
<p>You can customize the checkbox label and appearance.</p>
<p>For example:</p>
<p><strong>Subscribe &amp; Save - Get 20% off every delivery</strong></p>
<p>You can also style the surrounding container to make the subscription option more noticeable.</p>
<h3 id="step-5-save-and-publish">Step 5: Save and Publish<a class="prose-anchor" href="#step-5-save-and-publish" aria-label="Link to this section: Step 5: Save and Publish">#</a></h3>
<p>Save your changes and publish the checkout page.</p>
<p>When the customer checks the box, eligible one-time products in the cart are switched to their matching recurring offers.</p>
<p>When the checkbox is unchecked, the cart reverts to its original state.</p>
<figure class="prose-figure"><img src="/images/blog/checkout-champ-subscription-checkbox.webp" alt="Diagram of the Subscribe and Save checkbox: with the box unchecked the cart holds the one-time offer at $49.99, checking the box with the ID Subscribe switches it to the recurring offer at $39.99 every 30 days, and unchecking reverts the cart" width="1600" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Illustration: checking the box swaps eligible one-time items for their matching recurring offers.</figcaption></figure>
<p>There are a few important behaviors to understand:</p>
<ul>
<li>The recurring offer must share the same Base Product.</li>
<li>If the one-time product has variants, the selected variant is retained.</li>
<li>Products without matching recurring offers remain unchanged.</li>
<li>Products that are already recurring remain unchanged.</li>
</ul>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--tip" role="note" aria-label="Tip"><p class="prose-callout__label">Tip</p><strong>When should you use this method?</strong></p>
<p>Use the Subscribe &amp; Save checkbox when you want customers to upgrade their purchase without leaving the checkout page.</p>
</aside>
<h2 id="5-method-3-use-subscribe-and-save-profiles">5. Method 3: Use Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles<a class="prose-anchor" href="#5-method-3-use-subscribe-and-save-profiles" aria-label="Link to this section: 5. Method 3: Use Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles">#</a></h2>
<p>If you need to connect multiple one-time offers to recurring offers, Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles provide another way to manage the relationship.</p>
<p>Navigate to:</p>
<p><strong>CRM → Campaigns → Edit Campaign → Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles</strong></p>
<p>Create a profile and configure the following fields:</p>
<p><strong>Campaign Product:</strong> Select the one-time purchase offer.</p>
<p><strong>Recurring Product:</strong> Select the recurring offer that should replace the one-time purchase.</p>
<p><strong>Default Product:</strong> Choose which offer should be selected by default in the Subscribe &amp; Save element.</p>
<p><strong>Subscription Options:</strong> Configure the available delivery or billing frequencies.</p>
<p>The frequency options must include the words day, week, or month.</p>
<p>For example:</p>
<ul>
<li>Every 30 days</li>
<li>Every 60 days</li>
<li>Every 90 days</li>
</ul>
<figure class="prose-figure"><img src="/images/blog/checkout-champ-subscription-profile.webp" alt="Diagram of a Subscribe and Save Profile linking a one-time campaign product and a recurring product that share a Base Product, with the Default Product setting and subscription options of every 30, 60 and 90 days" width="1600" height="900" loading="lazy" decoding="async"><figcaption>Illustration: field names follow Checkout Champ's documentation. This is not a screenshot of the interface.</figcaption></figure>
<p>Both the one-time and recurring products must share the same Base Product.</p>
<p>You can also associate multiple one-time offers with one recurring offer, provided the required Base Product relationships are configured.</p>
<h3 id="why-use-subscribe-and-save-profiles">Why Use Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles?<a class="prose-anchor" href="#why-use-subscribe-and-save-profiles" aria-label="Link to this section: Why Use Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles?">#</a></h3>
<p>Profiles are useful when you want to manage subscription upgrades across different product sizes, bundles, or one-time offers.</p>
<p>They also support the Two Way Sub Subscription element discussed below.</p>
<h2 id="6-method-4-use-the-two-way-sub-subscription-element">6. Method 4: Use the Two Way Sub Subscription Element<a class="prose-anchor" href="#6-method-4-use-the-two-way-sub-subscription-element" aria-label="Link to this section: 6. Method 4: Use the Two Way Sub Subscription Element">#</a></h2>
<p>Checkout Champ Page Builder 2.0 also includes a native element called <strong>Two Way Sub Subscription</strong>.</p>
<p>This element can provide both subscription and one-time purchase options on the same page.</p>
<p>To use it:</p>
<ol>
<li>Open your checkout page in Page Builder 2.0.</li>
<li>Navigate to Building Blocks → Products.</li>
<li>Find the Two Way Sub Subscription element.</li>
<li>Drag it onto the page.</li>
<li>Style it according to your design.</li>
<li>Save and publish the page.</li>
</ol>
<p>This element uses the Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles configured in your campaign.</p>
<p>When customers upgrade to a subscription, the element is hidden and the order summary provides an option to revert to the one-time product.</p>
<p>This is useful when you want to give customers flexibility to switch between purchase types.</p>
<h2 id="7-can-you-customize-the-subscription-design-using-html-css-and-javascript">7. Can You Customize the Subscription Design Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript?<a class="prose-anchor" href="#7-can-you-customize-the-subscription-design-using-html-css-and-javascript" aria-label="Link to this section: 7. Can You Customize the Subscription Design Using HTML, CSS, and JavaScript?">#</a></h2>
<p>Yes, you can customize the appearance of your Checkout Champ pages using the supported page builder styling options and custom code.</p>
<p>For example, you may want a design like this:</p>
<p><strong>Choose Your Purchase Option</strong></p>
<ul>
<li>One-time purchase - $49.99</li>
<li>Subscribe &amp; Save - $39.99 / month</li>
</ul>
<p>You can create a more polished subscription selection experience with:</p>
<ul>
<li>Custom pricing cards.</li>
<li>Highlighted Subscribe &amp; Save options.</li>
<li>Discount labels.</li>
<li>Custom borders and background colors.</li>
<li>Responsive layouts.</li>
<li>Additional explanatory text.</li>
</ul>
<p>Start by using the native subscription elements and customizing them through the Style Manager.</p>
<p>If the native styling options are not sufficient, you can investigate custom CSS or HTML Blocks.</p>
<h3 id="important-don-t-replace-native-functionality-without-a-reason">Important: Don&#39;t Replace Native Functionality Without a Reason<a class="prose-anchor" href="#important-don-t-replace-native-functionality-without-a-reason" aria-label="Link to this section: Important: Don't Replace Native Functionality Without a Reason">#</a></h3>
<p>A custom radio button or checkbox does not automatically change the Checkout Champ cart to a recurring offer.</p>
<p>The native Subscribe &amp; Save functionality depends on the correct product relationships and element configuration.</p>
<p>If you build a completely custom subscription selector using HTML or JavaScript, you must also ensure that the selected option is connected to Checkout Champ&#39;s supported product-selection and cart behavior.</p>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--warning" role="note" aria-label="Warning"><p class="prose-callout__label">Warning</p><p>Avoid assuming that changing the visible price or checkbox state will change the actual billing configuration.</p>
</aside>
<h2 id="8-how-to-test-your-subscription-setup">8. How to Test Your Subscription Setup<a class="prose-anchor" href="#8-how-to-test-your-subscription-setup" aria-label="Link to this section: 8. How to Test Your Subscription Setup">#</a></h2>
<p>Before publishing a subscription funnel, test the complete customer journey.</p>
<h3 id="one-time-purchase-test">One-Time Purchase Test<a class="prose-anchor" href="#one-time-purchase-test" aria-label="Link to this section: One-Time Purchase Test">#</a></h3>
<ul>
<li>Confirm the one-time option is displayed.</li>
<li>Select the one-time offer.</li>
<li>Continue to checkout.</li>
<li>Verify the correct product and price.</li>
<li>Complete an approved test transaction.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="subscription-test">Subscription Test<a class="prose-anchor" href="#subscription-test" aria-label="Link to this section: Subscription Test">#</a></h3>
<ul>
<li>Select the subscription option.</li>
<li>Confirm the recurring offer is selected.</li>
<li>Verify the subscription price.</li>
<li>Check the billing frequency.</li>
<li>Complete an approved test transaction.</li>
<li>Verify that the order is recorded with the intended recurring configuration.</li>
</ul>
<h3 id="additional-checks">Additional Checks<a class="prose-anchor" href="#additional-checks" aria-label="Link to this section: Additional Checks">#</a></h3>
<ul>
<li>Confirm the checkout displays the correct subscription terms.</li>
<li>Verify the selected product and variant.</li>
<li>Check the order and customer records.</li>
<li>Verify the next billing schedule.</li>
<li>Test cancellation and subscription management workflows.</li>
<li>Check the frontend on desktop and mobile.</li>
</ul>
<aside class="prose-callout prose-callout--important" role="note" aria-label="Important"><p class="prose-callout__label">Important</p><p>Use an approved test environment or test payment process whenever possible. Don&#39;t assume a successful checkout page display proves that recurring billing is configured correctly.</p>
</aside>
<h2 id="9-common-subscription-setup-problems">9. Common Subscription Setup Problems<a class="prose-anchor" href="#9-common-subscription-setup-problems" aria-label="Link to this section: 9. Common Subscription Setup Problems">#</a></h2>
<p><strong>Problem 1: The Subscribe element isn&#39;t visible.</strong></p>
<p>Check whether the Base Product has an associated recurring offer. The native element may remain hidden if no recurring option is available.</p>
<p><strong>Problem 2: The Subscribe &amp; Save checkbox doesn&#39;t change the product.</strong></p>
<p>Verify that the checkbox ID is exactly <code>Subscribe</code>, and confirm that the one-time and recurring offers share the same Base Product.</p>
<p><strong>Problem 3: The wrong subscription option is selected.</strong></p>
<p>Review the Subscribe &amp; Save Profile and its recurring product configuration.</p>
<p><strong>Problem 4: The displayed price is incorrect.</strong></p>
<p>Check the recurring offer price and the profile configuration. Don&#39;t rely only on frontend text or manually entered pricing.</p>
<p><strong>Problem 5: The customer is charged incorrectly on rebilling.</strong></p>
<p>Review the recurring offer&#39;s billing schedule, price, and gateway configuration. Verify the subscription record and subsequent billing behavior using an approved test process.</p>
<h2 id="conclusion">Conclusion<a class="prose-anchor" href="#conclusion" aria-label="Link to this section: Conclusion">#</a></h2>
<p>Setting up subscriptions in Checkout Champ involves more than creating a recurring offer.</p>
<p>You need to connect the campaign products correctly, select the right frontend subscription element, and test the complete purchase journey.</p>
<p>For most implementations, the native Subscribe element, Subscribe &amp; Save checkbox, Subscribe &amp; Save Profiles, and Two Way Sub Subscription element provide the functionality needed without requiring a completely custom JavaScript solution.</p>
<p>Start with the native elements and use custom HTML, CSS, or JavaScript only when the design or functionality requires it.</p>
