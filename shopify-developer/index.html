In short

Meet Khunt is an ecommerce front-end developer who designs and builds Shopify stores for DTC brands and agencies. His Shopify work includes complete store redesigns for Baby Gains, Hanson of Sonoma and Aavilo, a collection page for Athletic Annex, and long-form landing pages for Riversol and Jadu Beauty. He builds custom theme sections in Liquid, HTML, CSS and JavaScript, turns Figma designs into working storefronts, and works directly with brands or white-label for agencies.

How I Work

  1. Step 1

    Brief & Review

    Your goals, the current store or Figma file, and the pages that matter most, reviewed before anything is quoted.

  2. Step 2

    Design

    Layouts for desktop and mobile, or your existing design checked for missing states and breakpoints.

  3. Step 3

    Build

    Custom theme sections in Liquid, HTML, CSS and JavaScript that your team can edit in the Shopify theme editor.

  4. Step 4

    Test & Launch

    Checked across devices and browsers, then launched, with fixes handled at the root cause.
