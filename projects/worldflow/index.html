WORDPRESS · WEBSITE DEVELOPMENT

WorldFlow

WordPress Website Development

WorldFlow manufactures UPVC, CPVC, SWR drainage, HDPE, column and casing pipe systems in India for agriculture, residential, commercial and industrial projects. Their website has to present a technical product range clearly, prove manufacturing quality and bring in dealer and project enquiries.

I built the website in WordPress: a homepage that walks through the range, the factory and the industries served, a page for each pipe system with specifications, certifications and FAQs, plus infrastructure, quality, careers, blog and contact pages, a dealer enquiry flow and a language switcher, with layouts that work on every screen size.
