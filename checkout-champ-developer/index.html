My Checkout Champ Expertise

More Than Just a Funnel Builder

A funnel on paid traffic is a chain where every link matters: the advertorial hands off to the right offer, the order form submits the right product, each upsell knows what came before it, the subscription bills on the right interval, and the pixel fires once per purchase. Most of the work is making that chain hold - and proving it does.

So the job isn’t done when the page looks right. I build the front end, configure the platform behind it, and walk the full customer journey with real test orders before anyone else has to.

  • Building from a brief

    A Figma design and approved copy are all I need to start. I build original pages for your brand and offer, not copies of someone else’s funnel.

  • HTML, CSS & JavaScript

    Hand-written markup, styles and scripts for package selectors, quantity and bundle logic, quiz flows, countdowns and anything the builder can’t do on its own.

  • Checkout Champ configuration

    Campaigns, products, offers and step paths set up so checkout, upsells, downsells and thank-you pages route correctly for every path a customer can take.

  • Subscription billing

    Subscription offers, billing intervals and pricing configured and verified with test transactions, so rebills match what the customer agreed to.

  • Tracking & attribution

    Pixels, UTMs and conversion events implemented and checked, and discrepancies traced to the cause instead of patched over.

  • A/B testing

    Variants for pages, offers, pricing and checkouts configured correctly, with tracking confirmed on each variant and the setup documented.

  • Funnelish, PageFly & Shopify Liquid

    Comfortable beyond Checkout Champ: funnels in Funnelish, pages in PageFly, and safe edits to Shopify theme files, sections and snippets in Liquid.

  • Full-journey QA

    Live test transactions that check products and pricing, subscriptions, upsells and downsells, tracking and the resulting order data - before launch, not after.
